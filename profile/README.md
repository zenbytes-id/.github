## About ZenBytes

**ZenBytes** (PT Mahakarya Digital Bisnis Integra) is a software house and IT consulting company based in Yogyakarta, Indonesia 🇮🇩, operating since 2021.

We help SMEs and startups solve real business problems through technology — not the other way around. We listen first, understand your workflow and budget, then build a solution that actually fits. Sometimes we even recommend *not* building an app if it's not the right time yet.

> *"Technology isn't the goal. Your growing business is."*
